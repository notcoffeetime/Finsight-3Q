The problem

Three apps.
Zero clarity.

You track tasks in one app, goals in another, and expenses in a spreadsheet. None of them talk to each other — and none of them tell you what to actually do next.

01
App fatigue
Switching between 3+ fragmented tools kills your focus and creates a false sense of productivity.
02
Static data
Traditional logs show what you did, not what you should do next. Hindsight isn't a strategy.
03
Mental load
Manual prioritization is slow and draining. You end up doing the easy things, not the important ones.

Everything in
one place.

Five tightly-integrated features that work together — because your life isn't modular.

Tasks

How it works

Set up in minutes.
Clarity from day one.

1

Log your life

Add tasks with deadlines, set financial goals, and log expenses as they happen. You stay the source of truth.

2

Firebase syncs instantly

Every entry is committed to Firestore in real time. Flexible data types match how you actually think about your life.

3

Groq AI thinks for you

Our backend triggers an AI call that prioritizes tasks, suggests budget cuts, and breaks goals into milestones — based on your real data.

4

Charts show the story

AI results write back to Firestore and trigger live UI updates — turning raw numbers into pie charts and priority lists you can act on.
